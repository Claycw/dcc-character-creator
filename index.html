Everyone starts as a regular Earth Human unless the GM permits one animal crawler in the group. Race and Class selection normally happen on Floor 3.

Animal crawlers start with 0 AI Favor and use animal background tables and Slice Attack instead of Unarmed Combat.

2. Backgrounds & skills

Choose one background in each of four life stages, then select exactly two of its three skills. Skills may not be selected twice from different backgrounds.

3. Starting combat

All Humans receive Unarmed Combat Rank 3; animal crawlers receive Slice Attack Rank 3. Choose one additional Rank 3 combat option.

Requires Intelligence 4 or higher. Grants 5 Standard Mana Potions.

4. Assign Stats

Use the standard array (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, once each) or the official random method (roll 1d6 in STR–CHA order, rerolling 1s). This creator supports manual entry for either method.

Starting Enhanced and Unenhanced values are identical. Stat modifiers use the Enhanced value.

5. Evade & Health

Your basic Evade check is d20 + DEX modifier. Evade is an Interrupt action and does not have a skill rank.

Health is tracked in 10 segments. Damage and healing follow the threshold rules in the Core Rulebook, p. 93, not simple one-point-per-hit-box subtraction.

6. Mana, Heal & Hotlist

All crawlers know Heal at Rank 1 (maximum). Heal is an Interrupt, costs 2 Mana, restores two Health Bar segments, and only targets yourself.

Hotlist (10 slots)

Enter quick-access spells, weapons, potions, or items. Empty slots are fine.

7. Movement, AI Favor & size

Spend 1 AI Favor to reroll a d20 before the GM announces the result (not a natural 1), or to gain one extra non-attack action per round.
